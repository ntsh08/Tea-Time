# Tea Time FAQ

## Why didn't I see a call?
Snooze might be on, your Mac might have been asleep, or you are on a different Wi-Fi network from the person who called. Tea Time only reaches Macs that are awake and on the same network. A call you miss leaves an orange dot and "Chai call" in the menu bar.

## Who can see my calls?
Only people on the same network who are running Tea Time. Nothing is sent over the internet.

## What does Tea Time send over the network?
A very small message with your display name and a random ID, so teammates know who called. There are no accounts, no tracking and no servers.

## Can someone spam everyone?
No. You can call once every 30 seconds, and anyone can snooze for an hour.

## Does it interrupt meetings?
It never takes your keyboard focus and it goes away on its own. If you are presenting, turn on **Snooze for 1 hour** from the menu. The call still shows in the menu bar, without the animation.

## Does it work in full screen?
Yes. The animation appears over full-screen apps.

## macOS says it cannot open the app. What do I do?
See [Opening it the first time](README.md#opening-it-the-first-time). It is a one-time step.

## macOS asked about the local network. Should I allow it?
Yes. Tea Time uses the local network to reach your teammates. If you chose Don't Allow by mistake, turn it back on in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network**.

## It says "No teammates nearby yet".
Check that your teammates have Tea Time open and are on the same network as you. Some office networks block this kind of traffic between devices. If yours does, please [open an issue](https://github.com/ntsh08/Tea-Time/issues) and say which network setup you have.

## Does it work on Windows or Linux?
Not yet. Tea Time is a Mac app for now.

## How do I turn off the sound?
Tea Time has a sound toggle in its menu. It also follows the macOS **Reduce Motion** setting and plays the calm version when that is on.

## How do I quit it?
Click the cup in the menu bar and choose **Quit Tea Time**.

## How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/ntsh08/Tea-Time/issues). Say what you did, what you expected, and your macOS version.
